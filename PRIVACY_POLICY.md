# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

OmSyncer is designed to work locally on your Android device. At this time, the app does not create user accounts, does not use analytics, does not display ads, and does not send your personal data to the developer's servers.

## Information the app processes

OmSyncer may process the following information on your device:

- Blood pressure and pulse measurements synced from a supported monitor
- Monitor-related metadata needed for syncing, such as the selected device and model
- Sync logs and sync captures generated for troubleshooting
- App preferences, such as Health Connect export and background sync settings

This information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

## How information is used

OmSyncer uses this information only to provide app features, including:

- Syncing measurements from a paired Bluetooth device
- Showing your saved measurements in the app
- Exporting measurements as CSV files when you request it
- Writing blood pressure and pulse data to Health Connect when you enable or trigger that feature
- Running optional nearby or background sync features when enabled
- Generating logs or sync captures for troubleshooting

## Data sharing

OmSyncer does not automatically share your data with the developer or other third parties.

Data may leave your device only in the following cases:

- You export a CSV file
- You export a sync log or sync capture
- You choose to share exported files using another app
- You grant permission for OmSyncer to write data to Health Connect

OmSyncer does not read Health Connect data in this version. It only writes blood pressure and pulse records when you explicitly enable or use that feature.

## Permissions

OmSyncer may request Android permissions such as Bluetooth access to connect to your monitor and sync measurements. If you enable nearby/background sync, the app may also use Bluetooth scan permission as required by Android.

These permissions are used only for app functionality on your device.

## Data retention and deletion

Your synced measurements and app data remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or export/share them yourself.

If you export data or write it to Health Connect, copies may remain in those locations until you remove them there.

## Children's privacy

OmSyncer is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future app releases. Any changes will be reflected in the updated policy text and date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the project repository or the app distribution page where you obtained OmSyncer.
